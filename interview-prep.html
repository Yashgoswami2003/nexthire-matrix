Interview Strategy Guide

Frequently Asked Questions About HR & Behavioral Interviews

1. How long should my "Tell me about yourself" answer be?

Aim for 60 to 90 seconds (around 120–160 words). Structure it using Present (your current role or degree), Past (key projects and measurable wins), and Future (why this exact role fits you).

2. What is the STAR method for answering project or behavioral questions?

STAR stands for Situation (context), Task (your responsibility), Action (the exact tools and steps you executed), and Result (the quantifiable impact).

3. How can freshers answer "Why should we hire you?" confidently?

Highlight your practical projects, problem-solving consistency, alignment with the job's required tech stack, and ability to ramp up quickly without excessive hand-holding.

4. How does the Draft Answer Scorer evaluate my response out of 10?

It analyzes word count pacing, action verbs, measurable numbers, and weak clichés to score your response out of 10 and generate an upgraded STAR-formatted replacement.

5. Are my interview answers stored on any external database?

No. Everything runs locally inside your browser window. Your interview scripts are never uploaded or saved on any server.
