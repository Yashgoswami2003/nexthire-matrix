                Easy Resume Formatting Guide
                
Frequently Asked Questions About Resume Format & Fonts

            


            

                

                    
                        1. Why do two-column resumes fail ATS software checks?
                        
                    
                    
Most company ATS tools read a page straight across from left to right. If you use two columns or tables, the software merges your left column words with your right column words, making your resume messy and unreadable.

                


                

                    
                        2. Which fonts are best for an ATS-friendly resume?
                        
                    
                    
Simple, clean fonts like Calibri, Arial, Roboto, Inter, Helvetica, and Georgia are supported by every hiring portal and look sharp on both desktop and mobile screens.

                


                

                    
                        3. Why do fancy bullet symbols cause problems?
                        
                    
                    
Decorative icons like stars (★), arrows (➤), or diamonds (◆) often break during PDF parsing and turn into weird characters. Always stick to standard round dots (•) or hyphens (-).

                


                

                    
                        4. What does the 1-Click Auto-Fix button do?
                        
                    
                    
It automatically replaces broken icons with standard bullets (•), cleans up extra wide spaces and hidden column tabs, and gives you clean text you can copy or download right away.

                


                

                    
                        5. Is my uploaded PDF resume stored on any server?
                        
                    
                    
No. Everything runs 100% locally inside your browser window. Your PDF and personal details are never uploaded anywhere.
