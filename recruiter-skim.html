Frequently Asked Questions About 6-Second Recruiter Skim Simulator

1. What is the 6-second recruiter resume rule?

Studies show that technical recruiters and hiring managers spend an average of only 6 seconds initially reviewing a resume to decide whether a candidate moves forward.

2. What is the F-Pattern eye-tracking layout?

Recruiters' eyes naturally move in an 'F' shape across a resume—scanning horizontally across the top header and summary, moving down the left margin for job titles, and scanning shorter lines to the right.

3. How can I make my resume pass the 6-second skim test?

Use clear section headers, bullet points with bold metrics (e.g., 'Reduced query latency by 42%'), clean professional fonts, and place your most impressive achievements near the top.

4. Does NextHire Matrix upload my resume text to a server?

No. The 6-second skim simulation and F-pattern heatmap rendering run 100% locally inside your browser's RAM for complete confidentiality.
