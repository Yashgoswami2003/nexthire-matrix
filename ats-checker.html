How ATS Resume Checkers Filter Your Resume

Applicant Tracking Systems (ATS) scan PDF resumes for specific job description keywords, technical stack relevance, and clean formatting. If your resume lacks primary job keywords, automated filters reject your application before a recruiter ever sees it.

Tips to Boost Your ATS Match Score Above 80%:

  • Use Standard Headings: Stick to simple section names like "Work Experience", "Education", and "Skills".
  • Match Technical Keywords Exactly: Include both acronyms and full terms (e.g., "AWS / Amazon Web Services", "CI/CD").
  • Avoid Complex Graphics: Tables, multi-column text boxes, and embedded images can fail ATS text parsing.
