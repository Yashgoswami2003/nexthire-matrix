How NextHire Matrix Protects Your Career Data

NextHire Matrix is engineered to eliminate data privacy risks found in traditional online career portals. Every tool—including ATS keyword scanning, LinkedIn bio auditing, salary calculations, CGPA conversions, and code analysis—executes directly inside your browser window without saving your documents on external servers.

Frequently Asked Questions

1. Do I need to create an account to use NextHire Matrix tools?

No. All 15 tools open instantly in your browser with zero sign-up, zero login walls, and zero subscription steps.

2. Is my resume PDF or personal text saved on your server?

No. PDF files and text inputs are processed locally inside your own browser window and are cleared automatically when you close or refresh the tab.

3. How accurate are the ATS Resume Checker, Salary Calculator, and CGPA Converter?

Our tools follow standard hiring benchmarks, Indian CTC tax/PF slabs, and official university conversion formulas (including CBSE, ICSE, and AKTU) to give reliable career and academic estimates.
