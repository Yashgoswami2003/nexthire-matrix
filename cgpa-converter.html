Frequently Asked Questions About CGPA, SGPA & Percentage Conversion

1. How do I convert AKTU CGPA to Percentage?

Under standard AKTU (Dr. A.P.J. Abdul Kalam Technical University) guidelines, multiply your 10-point CGPA by 10 to calculate your equivalent percentage (for example, 8.2 CGPA = 82.0%).

2. What is the official CBSE formula to convert CGPA to Percentage?

CBSE uses a multiplier of 9.5. Multiply your overall CGPA by 9.5 to get your approximate percentage (for example, 8.4 CGPA × 9.5 = 79.8%).

3. Can I convert my Percentage back into CGPA?

Yes. Simply enter your percentage in the Percentage box and the tool divides it by your selected board multiplier (9.5 for CBSE/ICSE or 10 for AKTU) to give your exact CGPA.

4. How do I calculate overall CGPA from semester-wise SGPA?

Switch to the "Semester SGPA → CGPA" tab and enter your SGPA for each completed semester. The calculator automatically computes your cumulative CGPA, equivalent percentage, and division.

5. How do I convert a 10-point CGPA to a 4.0 US GPA for Master's applications?

Use the "10-Point CGPA ↔ 4.0 GPA" tab to convert between Indian 10-point grading scales and 4.0 international GPA scales used by US and Canadian universities.
