                Outreach & Cover Letter Guide
                
Frequently Asked Questions About Cold Emails & Cover Letters

            


            

                

                    
                        1. How long should a cold email to a recruiter or hiring manager be?
                        
                    
                    
Keep cold emails between 80 and 130 words (around 4 to 5 concise lines). Busy hiring managers skim emails on mobile—state the role, your top skills, one measurable result, and your resume link immediately.

                


                

                    
                        2. What makes a LinkedIn referral request message get fast replies?
                        
                    
                    
Never just say "Hi, can you refer me?". Include the exact Job ID or posting URL, 2 quick points showing your match, and a direct link to your resume so they can submit your referral in under a minute.

                


                

                    
                        3. Which email subject lines get opened most by HR?
                        
                    
                    
Subject lines that combine the exact job role and a concrete proof point—such as "Application: React Developer — Built 3+ Production Apps"—outperform generic subject lines like "Resume Attached".

                


                

                    
                        4. How does the Draft Outreach Scorer rate my message out of 10?
                        
                    
                    
It evaluates message length, quantifiable proof, passive filler phrases, and clear call-to-action links to give a score out of 10 along with a rewritten version.

                


                

                    
                        5. Are my cover letters or email drafts stored anywhere?
                        
                    
                    
No. All generation and scoring execute 100% locally inside your browser window. None of your inputs are saved on any server.
