                Easy Outreach & Cover Letter Guide
                
Frequently Asked Questions About Cold Emails & Cover Letters

            


            

                

                    
                        1. How long should a cold email to a recruiter or HR be?
                        
                    
                    
Keep cold emails short—between 80 and 130 words (around 4 to 5 simple sentences). Busy recruiters read emails on their phones, so mention the exact role, your top skills, one strong project result, and your resume link right away.

                


                

                    
                        2. What makes a LinkedIn referral request message get fast replies?
                        
                    
                    
Never just write "Hi, can you refer me?". Always include the exact Job ID or role link, 2 short points showing why you fit the role, and a direct link to your resume so they can refer you in under 60 seconds.

                


                

                    
                        3. Which email subject lines get opened most by HR?
                        
                    
                    
Subject lines that combine the exact job title and a clear highlight—like "Application: React Developer — Built 3+ Live Apps"—get opened much more than generic subject lines like "Resume Attached".

                


                

                    
                        4. How does the Draft Scorer rate my email out of 10?
                        
                    
                    
It checks message length, real numbers/achievements, outdated formal phrases, and portfolio links to give your draft a score out of 10 along with a ready-to-use rewrite.

                


                

                    
                        5. Are my cover letters or email drafts stored anywhere?
                        
                    
                    
No. Everything runs 100% locally inside your browser window. None of your inputs or personal contact details are saved on any server.
