Online ATS Resume Checker & Keyword Scanner

NextHire Matrix provides an advanced ATS resume checker designed to help candidates optimize their resumes for modern Applicant Tracking Systems (ATS) like Workday, Greenhouse, Lever, and Taleo. Our instant PDF resume parser cross-references your skills against target job descriptions, detects header blind-spots, flags weak clichés, and prevents automated filters from rejecting your application.

Frequently Asked Questions (FAQ)

How accurate is this online ATS resume checker?

Our checker combines technical phrase recognition, weighted skill scoring, acronym expansion checks, and structural parsing rules to mirror how enterprise Applicant Tracking Systems rank candidates.

Is my PDF resume saved on your server?

No. NextHire Matrix processes all PDF text extractions locally inside your browser memory (RAM). Your private resume data is never uploaded or stored on any server.
