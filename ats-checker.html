Free Online ATS Resume Checker & Keyword Scanner

NextHire Matrix provides an advanced, free ATS resume checker designed to help candidates optimize their resumes for modern Applicant Tracking Systems (ATS). Our instant PDF resume parser cross-references your resume skills against target job descriptions to identify missing technical keywords, evaluate keyword density, and prevent automatic recruiter filters from rejecting your job application.

Key Features of Our Free Resume Scanner

🎯 Instant ATS Match Score

Calculates a precise relevance score comparing your PDF resume directly against target job requirements.

🔍 Missing Keywords Finder

Extracts hard technical skills, tools, and job phrases found in the JD that are absent from your resume text.

📑 Format & Layout Inspection

Audits section headings, contact information placement, and parseable PDF formatting safety.

🔒 100% Private Client-Side Scan

Processes text in your browser memory (RAM) so your private resume files are never uploaded to cloud servers.

Frequently Asked Questions (FAQ)

How accurate is this online ATS resume checker?

Our checker utilizes stop-word filtering and technical phrase recognition to match your PDF text against key job terms, accurately mirroring how top applicant tracking engines evaluate candidate match rates.

Is my PDF resume saved on your server?

No. NextHire Matrix processes all PDF text extractions locally inside your browser memory (RAM). Your private resume data never leaves your device.
