Why We Built NextHire Matrix

Most online resume checkers and career platforms lock basic ATS scores behind paywalls, collect candidate emails, or store uploaded resumes on external cloud databases.

NextHire Matrix changes this approach by running 100% locally inside your web browser. Whether you are scanning a resume, calculating monthly take-home pay from CTC, checking notice period dates, or practicing interview answers on your mic, your data never leaves your device.

4 Core Platform Pillars

  • Open Access For Everyone: Built for students, freshers, and working professionals with zero paywalls.
  • Privacy By Design: All resume checks and calculations run right on your device with zero server storage.
  • Unbiased Accuracy: Transparent scoring models aligned with real recruiter workflows and hiring standards.
  • Zero Login Friction: Open any utility and get instant results in seconds without creating an account.

Browser-Based Data Protection Guarantee

NextHire Matrix operates under strict on-device data isolation. We never store, process, monetize, or transmit user-submitted resumes, text content, or voice input to external servers. You retain 100% ownership of your career data.
