Why We Built NextHire Matrix

Most online resume checkers and career platforms lock basic ATS scores behind paywalls, harvest candidate emails, or store uploaded PDFs on cloud databases.

NextHire Matrix breaks this mold by running 100% locally inside your web browser. Whether you are scanning a PDF resume, calculating in-hand salary from CTC, testing code speed, or practicing interview answers on your mic, your data never leaves your device.

4 Core Platform Pillars

  • ⚡100% Free Forever: Built for students, freshers, and professionals with zero hidden paywalls.
  • 🔒Privacy By Design: All PDF parsing and calculations run in browser memory (RAM) with zero server storage.
  • 🎯Unbiased Accuracy: Transparent scoring models aligned with real ATS engines and recruiter workflows.
  • 🚀Zero Login Friction: Open any tool and get instant results in seconds without creating an account.
Under The Hood

How NextHire Matrix Works

1 Local Browser Extraction
