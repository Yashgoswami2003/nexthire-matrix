Frequently Asked Questions (Easy Guide)

1. How do I convert my CGPA into Percentage?

Just select your university or school board from the dropdown menu and enter your CGPA. The tool automatically uses your board's official formula (like × 10 for AKTU/IITs, × 9.5 for CBSE/ICSE, or (CGPA - 0.75) × 10 for VTU/Anna) and shows your percentage immediately.

2. Can I convert my Percentage back to CGPA?

Yes! Type your percentage in the Percentage box on the right, and the converter will reverse the calculation to show your exact CGPA on the left.

3. What if my university uses a different formula?

Select "Others / Custom Multiplier Formula" at the bottom of the dropdown list and enter your university's specific multiplier (like 9.25, 9.5, or 10) to get your exact percentage.

4. How do I calculate overall CGPA from semester SGPA?

Click on the "Semester SGPA → CGPA" tab and enter your SGPA for each completed semester. You can also use the Target CGPA Goal Planner slider to check what SGPA you need in your upcoming semesters.

5. How does the Subject-Wise Marks Calculator work?

Open the "Subject-Wise Marks" tab, enter your scored marks and total marks for each subject, and it will add everything up to show your total marks, overall percentage, and estimated SGPA.

6. How can I check how many marks I need in my next exam?

Use the "Target Score Predictor" tab. Enter the marks you already scored, the remaining exam marks, and your target percentage to see the exact marks needed in your final exams.

7. How do I convert Indian 10-point CGPA to 4.0 US GPA?

Switch to the "10-Point ↔ 4.0 US GPA" tab and enter your 10-point CGPA to see your equivalent 4.0 GPA for international university admissions.

8. What percentage or CGPA is needed for college placements?

Most IT and corporate recruiters require a minimum of 60% (6.0–6.5 CGPA), while top product companies usually look for 70% to 75% (7.5+ CGPA) with no active backlogs.
