3. Microphone Permission & Browser Preferences

Microphone Access (Speaking & Voice Coach): Our Speaking & Voice Coach tool asks for browser microphone access only when you tap "Start Recording" so it can measure speaking pace and filler words right inside your browser. No voice recordings are ever saved or transmitted anywhere.

Browser Preferences: Your browser may save basic display settings (such as Dark or Light theme mode and recent score comparisons) locally on your own device for convenience. You can erase all saved browser data anytime using the "Clear My Browser Data" button at the top of this page.

4. Advertising & Performance Insights

Advertising Partners: To keep NextHire Matrix accessible to everyone, third-party advertising networks such as Google AdSense may display relevant ads using standard browser cookies based on general browsing interests. You can customize or opt out of personalized ads anytime through Google Ads Settings or aboutads.info.

Anonymized Usability Insights: We measure general page speed and button interactions to keep the interface fast and reliable. Your personal text inputs, resumes, and calculations are never tracked or logged.

5. Your Data Control, Safety & External Links

You remain in complete control of your information at every step. Because NextHire Matrix does not store your resumes or personal documents on any server, closing or refreshing your browser tab immediately clears your active session.

Age Suitability & External Links: NextHire Matrix is designed for students, graduates, and job seekers aged 13 and above. Voluntary social sharing buttons (such as WhatsApp, LinkedIn, or Telegram) open external platforms governed by their own privacy policies.

6. Privacy Support & Direct Contact

We may update this Privacy Policy periodically as we add new career tools and improvements. For any privacy questions, feedback, or support inquiries, reach out directly to our support team at Nexthirematrix@gmail.com.

Simply click the "Clear My Browser Data" button at the top of this page to immediately reset all saved display preferences.
