Frequently Asked Questions About Salary & ESOP Simulator

1. How is monthly in-hand salary calculated under the 2026 tax regime?

Monthly in-hand salary is calculated by taking annual fixed base pay, deducting standard deductions and applicable income tax slabs under the 2026 New or Old tax regime, subtracting employee Provident Fund (PF), and dividing the net amount by 12.

2. How do 4-year ESOP vesting schedules work?

Standard startup and tech ESOP grants vest over 4 years, typically with a 1-year cliff followed by 25% vesting per year. This simulator automatically calculates your annual and cumulative ESOP wealth generation.

3. Is my salary and financial data stored on any server?

No. All calculations run 100% client-side inside your browser RAM. Your salary figures remain strictly confidential with zero data collection.
