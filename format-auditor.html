Why Multi-Column Layouts Break Enterprise ATS Parsers

Engineered by Yash Raj Goswami, NextHire Matrix uses PDF.js coordinate extraction to analyze layout structure. Traditional ATS algorithms (like Workday, Taleo, and Greenhouse) parse resumes sequentially from left to right. Multi-column tables or non-standard fonts can cause text overlapping, rendering your experience unreadable to automated screeners.
