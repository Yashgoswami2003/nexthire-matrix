Privacy Policy

Last Updated: November 2026 • Effective Date: Immediate

1. 100% In-Browser & Local Data Processing

NextHire Matrix operates entirely on client-side JavaScript logic. All resume PDFs, text content, Job Descriptions, salary inputs, speech recordings, and code snippets uploaded or pasted into our tools are processed locally inside your browser memory (RAM). No user files or confidential documents are ever transmitted, stored, saved, or logged to external web servers or cloud databases.

2. Analytics & Cookies

We use standard third-party analytics utilities (such as Microsoft Clarity and Google Analytics) strictly to monitor aggregated website usage metrics, page rendering performance, and UI responsiveness. These tools collect non-personally identifiable information (anonymized device type, screen size, referral URL, and aggregated navigation patterns). No resume document text or private form entries are captured in telemetry logs.

3. Advertising & Google AdSense DoubleClick Cookies

Third-party vendors, including Google, may use cookies to serve advertisements based on a user's prior visits to NextHire Matrix or other websites on the Internet. Google's use of advertising cookies enables it and its partners to serve targeted ads based on your navigation preferences. Users may opt out of personalized advertising by visiting Google Ads Settings.

4. User Data Rights (GDPR & CCPA Compliance)

Because NextHire Matrix does not harvest, store, or maintain user database accounts or server records, there is zero stored personal data to delete or export. All session data is immediately wiped from memory as soon as you close or refresh your web browser tab.

5. External Third-Party Links

Our web pages may contain voluntary sharing links to external third-party platforms (e.g., LinkedIn, WhatsApp). NextHire Matrix is not responsible for the privacy practices, content, or policies of external third-party web domains.

6. Contact Information & Support

If you have questions regarding this Privacy Policy or wish to report technical security inquiries, please contact us using our site feedback modal or email directly at support@nexthirematrix.com.
