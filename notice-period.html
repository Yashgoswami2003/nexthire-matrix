                Exit & Transition Guide
                
Frequently Asked Questions About Notice Period, LWD & Buyout

            


            

                

                    
                        1. How is the Last Working Day (LWD) calculated from the resignation date?
                        
                    
                    
Your notice period is counted in calendar days starting from the date you formally submit your resignation email or portal request, minus any approved Earned Leave offset or buyout days.

                


                

                    
                        2. How do Indian IT and corporate companies calculate notice period buyout?
                        
                    
                    
Most organizations calculate buyout recovery on Basic Pay or Gross Salary using the formula: (Monthly Salary ÷ 30) × Shortfall Days. Check your appointment letter to confirm whether Basic or Gross applies.

                


                

                    
                        3. Can Earned Leaves (EL) reduce my notice period duration?
                        
                    
                    
Depending on company policy and manager sign-off, accumulated Earned Leaves may be adjusted for an early release or paid out as leave encashment in your Full & Final (F&F) settlement.

                


                

                    
                        4. How many buffer days should I keep before joining my next company?
                        
                    
                    
Keeping a 3 to 7 day gap between your LWD and next joining date gives you breathing room to complete IT asset return, collect your relieving letter, and recharge.

                


                

                    
                        5. Are my resignation dates or salary numbers stored on any server?
                        
                    
                    
No. All date and buyout calculations run 100% locally inside your browser window. None of your figures are transmitted or saved anywhere.
