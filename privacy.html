1. 100% In-Browser & Local Data Processing

NextHire Matrix operates entirely on client-side JavaScript execution. All resume PDFs, text content, Job Descriptions, salary figures, speech recordings, and code snippets entered into our utilities are processed strictly inside your local web browser memory (RAM). No user files or confidential documents are ever transmitted, uploaded, stored, or logged to external web servers or cloud databases.

3. Microphone Permission & Browser LocalStorage

Microphone Access (Speech Coach): Our Speech & Voice Coach utility requests browser microphone permission solely when you click "Start Recording" to analyze speaking pace (WPM) and filler words locally via the Web Audio API. No audio files are ever saved or sent to any server.

Browser LocalStorage: We use your browser's built-in localStorage strictly to remember non-sensitive UI preferences (such as Dark/Light mode, your last ATS score comparison delta, and local review likes). You can erase this anytime using the "Clear My Local Data" button at the top of this page.

4. Advertising (Google AdSense DART Cookies) & Analytics

Google AdSense & DoubleClick Cookies: Third-party vendors, including Google, use cookies to serve advertisements based on a user's prior visits to NextHire Matrix or other websites on the Internet. Google's use of advertising cookies enables it and its partners to serve relevant ads. You may opt out of personalized advertising anytime by visiting Google Ads Settings or aboutads.info.

Microsoft Clarity & Telemetry: We use Microsoft Clarity to understand aggregated UI interactions (such as button clicks and page load speed) to improve usability. Sensitive text areas and resume inputs are never logged or transmitted in analytics events.

5. Your Privacy Rights (GDPR, CCPA & India DPDP Act)

Under global privacy standards—including the EU GDPR, California CCPA/CPRA, and India's Digital Personal Data Protection (DPDP) Act—you hold the right to access, delete, and opt out of data processing. Because NextHire Matrix maintains zero server databases of user files, closing your browser tab immediately clears all session data. We also honor browser Do Not Track (DNT) and Global Privacy Control (GPC) signals.

Children's Privacy & External Links: NextHire Matrix is intended for job seekers and students aged 13 and above and does not knowingly collect personal data from children under 13. Voluntary sharing links (such as LinkedIn or WhatsApp) lead to third-party platforms governed by their respective privacy policies.

Frequently Asked Privacy Questions

Can NextHire Matrix admins read my uploaded PDF resume?

No. Your PDF is parsed locally inside your own browser using client-side JavaScript. It is never uploaded to our servers, so nobody at NextHire Matrix can ever view or access your resume.

How do I delete my saved theme or local review data?

Simply click the "Clear My Local Data" button at the top of this page or clear your browser site data to immediately reset all local preferences.
